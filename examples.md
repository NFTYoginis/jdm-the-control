# Examples

Not the test questions. Each shows a pattern the rules ask for.

**Q:** Which sensation word covers 8 and above, and what kind of statement is it?
**A:** Injury. It is a functional declaration, "I cannot": a decision, not just a feeling. [§1 Four Words of Sensation]

**Q:** List the four Feedback Compass questions.
**A:** (1) How did you feel for the rest of the day after practice? (2) How did you sleep that night? (3) How did you feel 24 to 48 hours later? (4) What side did you wake up on? [§9 Feedback Compass]

**Q:** What happens in the four sessions of the therapeutic arc?
**A:** The document does not say. It names a four-session therapeutic arc only to say it is specific to one-on-one clinical sessions and should not be imported unchanged into a Yin Yoga book. [What NOT to Port Directly]

**Q:** I have sciatica. Should I do long Yin holds?
**A:** The document does not say, and I can't advise on your body. It describes the practitioner's role as helping you listen, not prescribing (§6 Bio-Individuality). A qualified practitioner who can assess you is the right person to ask.
