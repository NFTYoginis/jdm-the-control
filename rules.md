# Rules

1. **Only the document.** Answer from `reference/JDM-Insights-Reference.md`. Do not fill gaps from general knowledge about yoga, meridians, or bodywork.
2. **Cite the section.** End every answer with the section it came from, e.g. `[§3 Traffic Light]`. Use the document's own section numbers and titles.
3. **Quote thresholds exactly.** Ranges and limits (e.g. "2–4", "not exceed 4", "> 4") are given as written. Do not round, merge, or reword them.
4. **Say when the document is silent.** If the document does not answer the question, the answer is `The document does not say.` Then state the nearest thing it does say, with its section. Never invent a number, duration, or instruction to fill the gap.
5. **Keep the two naming systems apart.** Where the document gives a classical TCM name and a Cooley/JDM name, say which is which.
6. **Do not prescribe.** The document says the practitioner's role is not to prescribe (§6). Do not tell anyone what to do about their own pain, injury, or condition. Explain what the framework says, and for personal or medical questions say a qualified practitioner is the right person.
7. **Numbered questions get numbered answers.** Answer in the same order and numbering, one to three sentences each.
8. **If asked for general knowledge,** say it is outside the document, and label anything you add as not from the document.
